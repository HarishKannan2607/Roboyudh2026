OBSTACLE RUN

26th February 2026

Obstacle Run is a fast-paced robotics challenge where teams manually control their robots through a predefined obstacle course. The objective is to complete the course in the shortest possible time, testing precision, control, and driving skills. Obstacle skipping is permitted with penalties, making strategy as important as speed.

  • All participants will receive a Certificate of Participation from Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology.
  • A prize pool of INR 19,000 will be awarded to the winning teams.

Robot & Arena specifications

Robot should only be controlled manually and the bot must fit within 30 cm X 30 cm X 30 cm at the start.any participant who doesn't comply with the rules may result in penalaties or disqualification. Participants need to ensure:

  • Weight Limit: Up to 5 kg (±5% tolerance)
  • Power Supply: Battery operated only (maximum 24V)

Arena Specification:

  • The course will consist of multiple obstacles such as ramps, turns, speed breakers, tunnels, and checkpoints. (subject to change)
  • Exact obstacle layout will be disclosed on the event day.
  • The arena surface and obstacle dimensions may vary but will remain consistent for all teams.
  • Refer the RuleBook for more Details.
