Line Follower

26th February 2026

The Line Follower is an exciting event that tests the navigation and problem-solving skills of autonomous robots (Rovers) in a controlled maze environment. Robots are tasked with solving a maze using infrared sensors to follow a path and complete the maze in the shortest time possible. The event requires precision, quick decision-making, and efficient backtracking algorithms, with time being the key to victory

  • All participants will receive a Certificate of Participation from Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology.
  • A prize pool of INR 19,000 will be awarded to the winning teams.

Robot & Arena specifications

Dimensions of the bot must fit within a 25cm x 25cm x 25cm box.Weight limit must not exceed 3kgs.Any Participant who doesn't comply with the rules on rules may resuls in penalties or disqualification. Participants need to ensure:

  • Battery Voltage must be under 16.8 volts.
  • Robots must be autonomous and wireless (e.g., no Bluetooth, RF modules).
  • The robot must be built primarily by the team. Ready-made robots are not allowed

Arena Specification:

  • A white paper surface measuring 250cm in width and 250cm in length.
  • The track that robots must follow will be black, between 1.5cm and 2.5cm in width.
  • The timer starts when the robot crosses the start line and stops when it crosses the finish line. A stopwatch will be used for timing, with the judge’s time being final.
  • The maximum allowed time to solve the maze is 5 minutes.
  • Refer the RuleBook for more Details.
