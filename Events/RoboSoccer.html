ROBOSOCCER

26th February 2026

RoboSoccer is one of the most famous Robotic of event, a world robotics championship series. Teams of wireless bots chase a ball around a big-sized arena with the aim of kicking more goals than the opponent. This game gives the opportunity for youngsters to solve robotic challenges and build creative bots while learning science, technology, engineering, and math. The most rewarding part of designing bots is that students have fun and work together as a team, and learning occurs as naturally as breathing air.

  • All participants will receive a Certificate of Participation from Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology.
  • A prize pool of INR 44,000 will be awarded to the winning teams.

RoboSoccer Bot

The bot must fit inside a length of 30 cm (tolerance %5) . Maximum weight should not be more than 5Kgs (tolerance 5%) including battery for wirelessly controlled bots. Participants need to ensure:

  • Each team should have one robot for the full tournament. The substitution of robots during the competition within the team or with other teams is forbidden.
  • Batteries must be sealed
  • The electric voltage anywhere in the machine can used up to 12V DC at any point in time for each robot.
  • The robot should be manually controlled using a Remote-control system.
  • Refer the Rulebook for more details.

Tournament ball:The diameter of the ball will be 65.4mm(Standard tennis ball) (+- 5mm).
