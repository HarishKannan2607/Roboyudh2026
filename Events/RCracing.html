RC RACING

27th February 2026

Welcome to the RC Car Racing Championship, an electrifying competition that challenges speed, precision, and strategy. This event features multiple race formats, including drag racing, time attack, and autocross, pushing participants to showcase their vehicle engineering and driving skills. It's an exhilarating opportunity to compete against the best and claim ultimate victory.

  • All participants will receive a Certificate of Participation from Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology.
  • A prize pool of INR 25,000 will be awarded to the winning teams.

Robot & Arena specifications

Participants should not make an Intentional contact with other bots.leaving the track boundaries may resuls in penalties or disqualification. Participants need to ensure:

  • Type: Only electric RC cars are allowed.
  • Power Source: Vehicles can use any type of batteries, but not IC engines or non-electric drives.
  • Control: Vehicles must be operated using a wireless remote-control system.

Arena Specification:

  • Drag Racing: Straight-line track with marked lanes.
  • Time Attack: Circuit-style track with tight curves and straight sections.
  • Autocross: fastest time on a challenging section of track.
  • Minimum track width around 50cm (approx).
  • Refer the RuleBook for more Details.
